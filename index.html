Clear Memo

Your voice. Your control.

Record, organize, and revisit voice notes on your device.

Voice Recorder - Clear Memo supports recording, pause and resume, timestamp bookmarks, playback, favorites, folders, and sharing. Your recordings stay on your device unless you share them.

The Android app is in development and is not yet published on Google Play.
