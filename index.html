Clear Memo

Your voice. Your control.

Record, organize, and revisit voice notes on your device.

Voice Recorder - Clear Memo supports recording, pause and resume, timestamp bookmarks, measured live waveforms, playback speed controls, favorites, folders, and title-named audio sharing. Your recordings stay on your device unless you share them.

The Android app is in closed-test review on Google Play and is not yet available for public download.

Watch walkthroughs and updates on our Clear Memo Recorder YouTube channel.
