Clear Memo

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains how Voice Recorder - Clear Memo (Clear Memo), developed by omsai.k.dev, handles information. Contact: omsai.k.dev@gmail.com.

Your recordings stay on your device.

Clear Memo does not require an app account or automatically upload your audio. You decide when to share a recording. The Android app includes Google AdMob advertising, which uses network connections and processes information separately from your recordings.

Recordings and local information

Audio files, recording names, dates, durations, favorites, folders, timestamp bookmarks, and appearance preferences are stored in the app’s local files and database. Clear Memo does not operate a cloud storage, transcription, or automatic recording-upload service. We do not receive your recordings or library contents through the app.

Permissions

Microphone access is requested when you start recording. An active session may continue in the background or while the screen is locked. Android uses a foreground service with a recording indicator; notification permission controls the notification’s visibility in the notification drawer. You can manage permissions in your device settings. Denying microphone access prevents recording but does not prevent browsing saved recordings.

Sharing

Sharing is initiated by you through the device’s share sheet. The selected audio is provided to the destination app or service you choose. Its handling of the file is governed by its own privacy practices. Clear Memo does not control copies you export or share.

Advertising and Google services

The Android app integrates Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform for advertising and consent choices. Current development and preview builds request Google test advertisements. Live advertisements are not enabled yet.

Google’s advertising SDK collects and shares information such as IP address, device and account identifiers (including advertising identifiers where available), ad and app interactions, and diagnostic information. IP addresses may be used to estimate general location. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement and analytics, and fraud prevention. SDK processing can occur even when a request does not result in a visible advertisement.

Clear Memo does not pass your audio, recording names, folders, or bookmarks to the advertising SDK. We have no separate app analytics service. Advertising requests are configured as non-personalized; this does not eliminate SDK data processing or applicable consent requirements.

For details, see Google’s Privacy Policy, how Google uses information for advertising, and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures.

Your advertising choices

Where required, Google’s consent interface asks for your choices before ads are requested. When Google requires a privacy-options entry, you can revisit available choices through Settings → Ad privacy options. Choices and availability depend on your region and Google’s configured messages. You can also manage advertising-related controls in Android settings. If consent setup fails or Google indicates ads cannot be requested, Clear Memo leaves ads disabled.

Retention, deletion, and backups

Local recordings remain until you delete them or app data is removed. Deleting a recording also removes its associated bookmarks; deletion that cannot finish is retried on a later launch. Uninstalling or clearing app data may remove your local library. Android app backup is disabled in Clear Memo’s configuration. Files you export and any copies held by other apps are outside Clear Memo’s control.

We cannot retrieve or delete local files remotely. Use the app or your device controls to remove them. Google controls retention of information processed by its services under its policies and your applicable choices; Clear Memo does not set a retention period for that information.

Security

Recordings use app-local storage. Clear Memo does not add its own audio encryption, and no storage or transmission method is guaranteed secure. Protect your device and export important recordings to a location you trust. An active recording is not guaranteed to survive force-stop, shutdown, or process termination.

Contact and privacy requests

Email omsai.k.dev@gmail.com with questions or privacy requests. If you contact us, your email provider delivers the information you include so we can respond. Avoid sending private recordings or other sensitive information unless needed for your request. Requests concerning Google-held information can also be directed to Google using its privacy resources.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its date when our information practices change, including before enabling materially different features or advertising behavior.
